## Failure patterns

### Pattern 1 – Non-hatchback body variants kept as hatchback results
Affected: 35 of 237 rows (14.8%); 5 of 30 sampled (idx 41 Honda Civic sedan, 65 Seat Ibiza polocabrio, 130 Honda Civic Coupe, 146 Peugeot 207 CC kabriolet, 179 Škoda Fabia Combi).
Cause: the sub-type hatchback is enforced only by subtype.models (make+model name matching); subtype.variants is empty so body-variant words (combi, sw, st, sedan, coupe, kabriolet, aerodeck, picasso, berlingo, verso) are never rejected — src/research/candidates.mjs:173-178.
Root cause: applyCalibration drops every variant word that is not literally found in the calibration titles — src/research/decision.mjs:210 — and truncates multi-word model names to their first two words, producing junk models such as prodam citroen / prodam citroen c1 that match any Prodám Citroén title (for example prodam citroen berlingo, Citroen C4 grand Picasso) — src/research/decision.mjs:214-215.
Acceptance bypass: purposePass returns true for any harvested sweep item without reading the page — src/research/acceptance.mjs:229 — so the reported 100% Page shows the asked hatchback score does not actually verify hatchback on any of the 30 inspected deliverables (all 30 are harvested sweep items).

### Pattern 2 – Wanted ad kept
Affected: 1 row (Toyota Yaris, sháním).
Cause: the WANTED regex — src/research/itemgate.mjs:8 — lacks Czech/Slovak wanted verbs (sháním, hledám, koupím, ...). Fixed in this commit. Verified live: the page is a wanted ad (Toyota Yaris, sháním).

### Pattern 3 – Parts ad kept
Affected: 1 row (Motor dacia sandero, engine block for parts).
Cause: the kind not list is [parts, accessories, kola]; motor (an engine sold alone) is not included. applyCalibration keeps a not word only if it appears in >= 2 calibration titles — src/research/decision.mjs:209 — so rare part words are dropped, and screenItem only rejects via the not list — src/research/itemgate.mjs:49-50.

### Pattern 4 – Distance to Prague not measured / not enforced
Affected: 184 of 237 rows (78%) lack a distanceKm value.
Cause: measureDistances only geocodes up to MAX_DISTANCE_LOOKUPS rows and skips rows without a stated place or a geocodable point; rows without a measured distance are never rejected — src/research/engine.mjs:467-513. So within 300 km from Prague is not applied to most kept rows (the log line distance from prague: 60 listing(s) measured, 0 farther than 300 km covers only 60 rows).

### Pattern 5 – Instalment / deposit ads kept
Affected: 2 rows (Škoda Fabia III 1.4 TDI | Auto na splátky | Bez registrů, ŠKODA FABIA III 1.4 TDi na SPLÁTKY BEZ REGISTRU).
Cause: implausiblyLow — src/research/candidates.mjs:211-221 — removes only prices far below the market median; instalment ads are priced at the deposit, not the car price.

### Pattern 6 – Acceptance kind check bypass
Affected: 30 of 30 inspected deliverables.
Cause: purposePass returns true for any harvested sweep item without reading the page — src/research/acceptance.mjs:229. The acceptance score of 1.0 is therefore inflated relative to the actual body-type compliance.

## Fix applied
Narrow, single-function fix for Pattern 2: extended the WANTED regex in src/research/itemgate.mjs to cover Czech/Slovak wanted verbs (sháním, sháníme, sháníte, shánět, hledáme, hledáte, hledají, koupím, koupíme, koupíte, kupujeme, kupujete, kupují, poptáváme, poptáváte, poptávají). screenItem now rejects Toyota Yaris, sháním as a wanted ad, not an offer.
Test added: tests/research/itemgate.test.ts (new it() refuses a Czech car wanted ad that says sháním). It fails before the fix and passes after.
Verification: npx vitest run tests/research/itemgate.test.ts tests/research/candidates.test.ts tests/research/acceptance.test.ts -> 3 test files passed, 70 tests passed.
